# DynaPrompt Test Prompts Dataset
# These prompts are designed to test compositional understanding and attribute binding
# Format: one prompt per line

# Multi-object with colors
A blue cat sitting on a red chair with a yellow ball
A golden retriever playing with a red ball in a snowy park
A green apple next to a blue bowl on a wooden table

# Attribute binding (color + object)
A purple elephant wearing a pink hat
A silver car parked next to a golden bicycle
A white dog with brown spots sitting under an orange umbrella

# Compositional scenes (multiple objects + locations)
A cat on top of a laptop next to a coffee cup on a desk
A bird perched on a fence with mountains in the background
A teddy bear sitting on a bed with a book and glasses nearby

# Complex multi-attribute prompts
A small red bird eating seeds from a blue feeder in a garden
A wooden boat with white sails floating on a calm blue lake at sunset
A black and white cow grazing in a green field under cloudy sky

# Challenging prompts (known to fail on vanilla SD)
Three colorful balloons - one red, one blue, one yellow - floating in the sky
A striped cat and a spotted dog playing together in a park
Two cups on a table - one filled with coffee, one filled with tea
